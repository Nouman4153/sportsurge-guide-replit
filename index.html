Sportsurge: A Simple Guide to Live Sports Schedules, Scores and Guides

Welcome to sportsurge, a sports schedule and guide hub for fans in the United States. Here you will find today’s NFL, NBA, UFC, soccer, F1, MLB, NHL and boxing matches with kickoff times in US Eastern Time, teams and official broadcast information in one place.

Quick answer: Sportsurge tells you what is on today, tomorrow and this weekend. Pick your sport, check the time in Eastern Time, and get ready before the game starts. You do not need an account to check a schedule.

What Is Sportsurge?

Sportsurge is an online sports hub that brings live sports information together in one place. Its job is simple: it tells you what is on today, tomorrow and this weekend, with separate lists for NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, UFC, boxing, F1, soccer and rugby. Big events are marked clearly, such as playoffs, title fights and derby matches, and each sport has its own guide so new fans can follow along too.

Sportsurge is not a TV channel. It is a guide that helps you get to the game. You check the schedule here, then watch the game through your official broadcaster or streaming service. That is why it is easy to use: no sign up, no long form and no extra step.

How to Use Sportsurge in 3 Simple Steps

  1. Pick your sport. Choose NFL, NBA, UFC, soccer, F1, MLB, NHL, boxing or rugby from the menu. Each sport has its own list. For the full NFL schedule, open the Sportsurge NFL guide. For basketball, open the Sportsurge NBA guide. For fights, open the Sportsurge UFC guide.
  2. Check today’s schedule. Each match shows the date, kickoff time and both teams. Times are in US Eastern Time. If you are in Pacific Time, take away three hours. If you are in Central Time, take away one hour. If you are in Mountain Time, take away two hours.
  3. Get ready before the game. For big games, arrive 10 to 15 minutes early. Lineups, injuries and last-minute changes often appear just before kickoff. Keep refreshing the schedule, because times can change, especially after a rain delay or overtime.

One small habit can save you a lot of time: put the game you want to watch in your phone calendar. That way you will remember it and you will not miss the start.

Check Today’s Sportsurge Schedule

Sportsurge NFL

The NFL is the biggest sport in America and its schedule is searched more than any other. On Sundays, games start at 1 PM Eastern, then a second set starts around 4 PM Eastern, and Sunday Night Football starts at 8 PM Eastern. Monday Night Football starts at 8 PM Eastern too.

In the Sportsurge NFL guide you will find each week’s games, bye weeks and the playoff picture. If you only follow one team, search for that team name and you will see its next three or four games together in one list.

You can watch the NFL through official services such as NFL Plus, CBS, Fox, NBC, ESPN and Amazon Prime Video, which shows Thursday Night Football. Pick the one that fits your cable or streaming plan. Sportsurge tells you when the game is on. Where you watch it depends on your plan.

Fantasy football players need the schedule even more. If you know whether your player plays Sunday at 1 PM or Monday night, you can set your lineup on time. Check the schedule on Sunday morning before you lock your team.

Sportsurge NBA

The NBA season runs from October to April and there are five to ten games almost every night. That is why NBA fans need to check the schedule daily. In the Sportsurge NBA guide you will find today’s games, their times and both teams in one clear list.

The big days are Christmas Day, Martin Luther King Day and the playoffs. In the playoffs the schedule changes. A series follows a 2-2-1-1-1 format and a Game 7 can happen on any night. Check the schedule every day during the playoffs.

You can watch the NBA through NBA League Pass, ESPN, TNT and ABC. League Pass is best if you want to watch a team from outside your city. Local games are often on a local channel. Highlights are ready one or two hours after the game, but a live game feels different, especially when the score is tied in the last two minutes.

Sportsurge UFC, Soccer, F1 and More

UFC

UFC Fight Night is often on Saturday night and pay-per-view events are on Saturday too. Prelims start at 8 PM Eastern and the main card starts at 10 PM Eastern. In the Sportsurge UFC guide you will find the next event card, the main event, the co-main event and the prelim fights. In the United States, the official way to watch the UFC is ESPN Plus.

Soccer

Premier League games start at 7.30 AM Eastern on Saturday, then at 10 AM and 12.30 PM. Champions League games are on Tuesday and Wednesday at 12.45 PM and 3 PM Eastern. In the soccer guide you will find separate lists for the Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1 and the Champions League.

F1

The Formula 1 calendar runs from March to December and each race is in a different country, so the race time changes every week. In the Sportsurge F1 guide you will find the next Grand Prix with practice, qualifying and race times listed separately. In the United States, F1 is shown on ESPN.

MLB, NHL and Boxing

Baseball runs from April to October, with games often at 7 PM Eastern. Hockey runs from October to April, with games often at 7 PM Eastern and the Stanley Cup Playoffs from April to June. Boxing big fights happen once or twice a month, often on Saturday night at 9 PM or 10 PM Eastern. See the baseball guide, the NHL guide and the boxing guide on Sportsurge.

Is Sportsurge Safe and Legal to Use?

Checking a schedule, a score or a guide is safe. That information is public and it is on every sports site. For watching a live game, always choose an official broadcaster. Each league has official partners and only they have the right to show the game. If a site promises every game free with no rights at all, stay away from it. Such sites often have pop-ups, fake buttons and malware.

Sportsurge is not affiliated with any league, team or broadcaster. The NFL, NBA, UFC, FIFA, Formula 1 and their logos belong to their owners. The names are used here only so you know which sport is being discussed.

Watch games only through a service you know, such as ESPN, NBC, Peacock, Paramount Plus, DAZN or a league app. If any site asks you to download software or allow notifications, do not allow it. Keep your ad blocker and antivirus updated.

Five Tips So You Never Miss a Game

  1. Check Friday for the weekend. Look at the next three days on Friday night.
  2. Know your time zone. All times are Eastern Time. Pacific Time is three hours behind, Central Time is one hour behind and Mountain Time is two hours behind.
  3. Set a reminder for big games. The Super Bowl, the NBA Finals, a UFC title fight and the Champions League final happen once a year.
  4. Check lineups and injuries. Lineups appear about one hour before the game.
  5. Focus on one game. When five games run at the same time, pick one game, watch it fully and check the other scores on your phone.
